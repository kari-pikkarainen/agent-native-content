# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of technical
expertise. Disagreement about methods and evidence is welcome; hostility
toward people is not.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas, code, and experiments rather than people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and research-integrity constraints.
- Accept correction and disclose mistakes, negative results, and conflicts of
  interest when they matter to the work.
- Do not publish credentials, private source material, personal information,
  or surveillance-grade derived data in issues or pull requests.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and
unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and
should protect reporters' privacy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/kari-pikkarainen). For a
security-sensitive concern, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting channel. Do not open a public issue that identifies a target of
harassment or exposes private information.
